COOPERATE WITHOUT OVERSHARING

One shortage.
Different business realities.

Factories know their own costs and tolerances. TradeOff coordinates the signals they approve, while each participant checks whether the outcome works locally.

01 / BOUNDARIES

Share the signal.
Retain the source.

Separate service users and private databases. The coordinator receives requests, priorities, and bounded acceptance feedback.

Enforced on the LinuxONE VM
02 / ALLOCATION

Turn disagreement
into constraints.

A rejected proposal becomes a minimum quantity for the next solve. Two rounds keep the negotiation legible and bounded.

Exact integer optimization
03 / ACCOUNTABILITY

Learn locally.
Check every action.

A trained model prioritizes validation order. The controller and solver enforce rules; a hash-linked audit records the actions.

No external AI API required
